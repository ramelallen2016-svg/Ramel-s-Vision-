§Operating principle
Verified only
Phone numbers, ratings, and review counts come straight from the listing. Nothing is generated.
Absence is stated carefully
"No website found in sources checked" — never "they have no website."
Owners are never guessed
If no source names a decision maker, the field reads unverified. Ask on the call.
Scoring is explainable
Every point in the 100-point model traces to a category you can see.

§02On phone numbers

Most listings carry a phone number, but not all of them do. With Require a phone number switched on, anything without a verified number is dropped before scoring — so every file you open is one you can dial. Numbers come from the listing itself and are never generated or inferred.
