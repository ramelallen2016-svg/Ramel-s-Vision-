§03Owner names and emails — read this

Business listings carry the company name, phone, address, reviews, and website. They almost never carry the owner's personal name, and only sometimes carry an email. This app fills those fields when a real source has them and says so plainly when it doesn't — because calling a business and using the wrong name kills the call in three seconds.

How to get the owner's name in 10 seconds, every time:

"Hey, quick one — who handles the website and marketing side over there?"

Whoever picks up will either say "that's me" or hand you a name. Now you have the decision maker and a reason to be transferred. That works better than any scraped field ever will.
